## New car warranty
- New cars carry the manufacturer's standard warranty: typically 3 years or 100,000 km, whichever comes first (varies by brand and model — confirm on the invoice).
- Hybrid battery warranty is usually longer (e.g. 8 years). Check the specific model's warranty booklet.
- Warranty requires periodic service to be done on time at an authorised workshop with genuine parts.
- Warranty does not cover normal wear items (tyres, wiper blades, brake pads, clutch), accident damage, or damage from unauthorised modifications.

## Certified used cars
- Our certified used cars come with a 6-month / 10,000 km limited powertrain warranty and a 150-point inspection report.
- Each used car's history and inspection report is available on request.

## Service schedule (petrol cars)
- First service: 1,000 km or 1 month (free labour / inspection).
- Periodic service: every 5,000 km or 6 months (engine oil + oil filter change, inspection).
- Major service: every 20,000 km (oil, all filters, spark plugs, brake check).
- Hybrids follow a similar interval; coolant and brake fluid are changed as per the manufacturer's schedule.

## How to book a service
- Book through WhatsApp, the website chat, or by calling the showroom.
- Tell us your car model, registration number, the service you want and your preferred date and time.
- You will get a reminder the day before, and a message when the car is ready for pickup.

## Common questions
- "Is my car ready?" — Share your job number (e.g. JOB-1043) and we'll check the live status.
- "Do you do pick and drop?" — Yes, pick-up and drop service is available within the city for an extra charge.
- "Can I get a cost estimate before coming?" — Yes, tell us the service type and car model for an estimate.
- "Do you use genuine parts?" — Authorised service uses genuine parts. Cheaper alternative parts are available on request for out-of-warranty cars.
