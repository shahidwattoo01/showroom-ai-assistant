## Documents needed to buy a car
- Original CNIC of the buyer (and a copy).
- Proof of address (recent utility bill).
- For company purchase: NTN certificate and an authority letter.
- For bank financing: last 6 months bank statement and salary slip / proof of income.
- Booking is confirmed only after the booking amount is received and a receipt is issued.

## Booking and delivery
- A new car is booked against a booking order and advance payment.
- Delivery time depends on the model and colour; popular colours are usually available immediately from stock.
- The final on-road price includes ex-showroom price + freight + registration + token tax + insurance (first year). The exact breakdown is given on the quotation.

## Test drive policy
- Test drives are available at the showroom or at the customer's home by appointment.
- Please bring a valid driving licence. The driver must be 18+ with a valid licence.

## Trade-in (exchange)
- We accept your old car in exchange. Bring the car, original registration book and your CNIC for an on-the-spot valuation.
- The final exchange value depends on the model, year, mileage and condition after inspection.

## Payment
- We accept bank transfer, pay order and cash (as per legal limits). Card payments are accepted at the counter.
- The showroom never asks for card numbers, CNIC or passwords over chat or phone. Payments are made in person or via an official bank account/payment link only.
